# SportAddict Gear Service

> Professional sports equipment rental, repair, and servicing for athletes, clubs, and teams across Malaysia.

**[Live Demo →](https://p1-seven-ruby.vercel.app/)**

---

## About

SportAddict is a modern, fully responsive sports gear service landing page. The site presents a complete rental and servicing platform for athletes — from casual players to full sports academies — with a dark neon aesthetic, smooth scroll animations, and sport-specific service panels.

---

## Features

### Six Core Services
| Service | What It Covers |
|---|---|
| Equipment Rental | Football boots, badminton rackets, tennis rackets, bicycles, gym equipment — daily, weekly, or monthly |
| Gear Repair | Expert fix for damaged rackets, boots, bike frames, and gym machines — same-day minor repairs |
| Deep Cleaning | Anti-bacterial treatment, deodorizing, and appearance restoration for all gear types |
| Performance Tuning | Racket restringing, boot stud replacement, bike derailleur calibration |
| Customization & Setup | Custom grip tape, nameplate engraving, color wraps, ergonomic fitting to playing style |
| Maintenance Plans | Monthly packages for clubs and school teams with priority service and fleet discounts |

### Sport-Specific Panels
Interactive tabbed section with tailored rental options and services per sport:
- **⚽ Football** — boots from RM15/day (re-stud, deep cleaning, custom colorway)
- **🏸 Badminton** — rackets from RM10/day (restringing to ±0.5 lbs tension accuracy)
- **🎾 Tennis** — rackets from RM12/day (overgrip, lead tape, vibration dampener setup)
- **🚴 Cycling** — road/MTB bikes from RM35/day (full tune-up, brake & gear calibration)
- **🏋️ Gym Equipment** — from RM25/day (dumbbells, barbells, benches, cardio machines)

### UI & Interactions
- Scroll-triggered animated stat counters (2,000+ athletes, 500+ gear items, 98% satisfaction, 24h turnaround)
- Intersection Observer-based reveal animations across all sections
- Sticky transparent navbar that transitions to frosted glass on scroll
- Responsive mobile menu with smooth height transition
- Floating sport icons in hero background with staggered animation
- Neon green dark theme with gradient text accents

### Page Sections
1. **Hero** — Headline, animated stats bar, dual CTA buttons
2. **Services** — 6 service cards with hover icon swap and feature checklist
3. **Why Choose Us** — 6 trust pillars (ISO Certified, 30-Day Guarantee, 5-Star Rated, Same-Day Service)
4. **How It Works** — 3-step process with connecting timeline (Choose → Prepare → Perform)
5. **Sports** — Tabbed sport selector with per-sport service list and pricing
6. **Testimonials** — 6 athlete reviews, 4.9/5 overall rating from 2,000+ reviews
7. **CTA** — Booking call-to-action with phone, email, and location
8. **Footer** — Service links, sport links, contact hours, social media
